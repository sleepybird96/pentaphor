## Description
STACK YOUR PROGRESS.
Small steps. Growing stronger.

Turn the things you want to do into quests. Reading, running, cooking, or something entirely your own: you choose the action, the art, and the growth it brings.

BUILD FIVE PARAMETERS
Grow Stamina, Knowledge, Perseverance, Charm, and Courage. Assign up to 2 points to each quest and watch your pentagon change as your progress adds up.

FIND YOUR OWN PACE
Set weekly or monthly goals, or make a one-time quest with no deadline. You don't have to do everything every day. Consecutive weekly or monthly goals earn a Perseverance bonus.

SEE WHAT YOU'VE BUILT
A weekly recap celebrates your completed actions and growth. Your week starts Monday at midnight, with time until Monday at 9 AM to record the previous week.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from 60 illustrated actions. Add notes, set reminders for selected days and a time, and pause or archive quests. Reminders stop for the period once a recurring goal is reached, or when a one-time quest is complete.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Your entries stay on your device. Manually back up to your own iCloud Drive and restore from a backup file. Automatic syncing is not provided.

GROW BEYOND THE LIMITS
Free mode supports 8 active quests and displays up to 99 in each parameter. Growth beyond 99 is still saved. CHALLENGE is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited active quests and reveals all your earned parameter points. No subscription.

Available in Korean and English.
